How to Play

Computer Keyboard Mapping

White Keys: C D E F G A B N (Octave C)
Black Keys: Shift + C (C#) · Shift + D (D#) · Shift + F (F#) · Shift + G (G#) · Shift + A (A#)

Shift Octaves & Sustain

Press Z to shift down an octave, and X to shift up.
Hold Space for everlasting sustain (like a real pedal).

Mixer & Stacks

Use the Settings gear to open the mixer. Change voices, add reverb, and transpose.
Click the + Plus button to add more pianos stacked on top of each other. Use the D-Pad in the settings to move the newest piano.

Record & Save

Click Start Recording in the settings to capture your audio. Click again to stop and download.
Click Save to My Melodies to keep a history in your account.
