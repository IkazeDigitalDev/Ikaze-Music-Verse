Meet the founder.

NIYIBIGIRA Theoneste

A musician and pianist, developer, creator and technology educator whose work brings together music, learning and digital innovation. His portfolio features Rwanda Tech Learning Hub, Theo Learn Wise AI, responsive web projects, Firebase and cloud learning, AI-assisted education, piano and choir leadership, journalism, arts and design, and technology training. His mission is to use technology, education and music to create useful solutions and empower people to develop their potential.

Ikaze Music Club was founded in November 2026 at TTC Muhanga, with a mission to nurture musical ability, build practical performance skills and help prepare musicians for opportunities in the job market.
